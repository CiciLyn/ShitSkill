# Navigation Rules

## Explain Non-Obvious Transitions

Give a reason and supported edge for every non-obvious transition between files, modules, functions, concepts, or abstraction levels, and trace concrete runtime behavior through indirection until its observable effect or relevant system boundary.

## Explain Why It Matters

Explain why a component matters to the current goal before diving into its implementation.

## Stay Goal-Relevant

Do not explore a branch that does not advance the current goal without explaining why the detour is necessary.

## Maintain Zoom Coherence

Move from the global picture through the relevant path to local detail and back to the conclusion without unexplained abstraction jumps.
